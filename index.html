A LITTLE CONTEXT

What do these labels mean?

● Favourable dated record

A documented favourable inspection or hygiene audit, which may be several years old. Reinstatement alone does not establish a positive hygiene result. Always check the scheme, source and date.

● Historical action

A past enforcement action was documented. Its later outcome is shown where available.

● Suspension recorded

A suspension was documented. We only describe a suspension as current when there is sufficiently recent official evidence.

● Outcome unverified

The available evidence does not establish a current outcome. This is not an approval or safety rating.
